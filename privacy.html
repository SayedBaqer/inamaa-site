iNamaa Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your financial data stays on your device

Accounts, transactions, budgets, loans and categories are stored locally on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to our servers.

What we collect

If you choose to sign in, we collect your email address and a password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication) to provide your account. We do not collect your financial data, contacts, location or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you.

Clipboard and files

When you tap the paste button, the app reads your clipboard once to fill in a transaction. Statement files you import are processed on the device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Third-party services

Google Firebase (sign-in and announcements) and Apple (purchases). Their privacy policies apply to the data they process.

Deleting your data

Use Settings, then Delete account, to remove your account and its cloud data. Deleting the app removes all data stored on the device.

Children

iNamaa is not directed at children under 13.
