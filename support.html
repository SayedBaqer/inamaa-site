Common questions

Where is my data stored? On your device. Signing in is optional.

How do I restore Pro? Open any Pro feature, then tap "Restore purchases".

How do I delete my account? Settings, then Delete account.

How do I cancel my subscription? iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, iNamaa Pro.
